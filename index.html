About Me

I am a Research Engineer in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the National University of Singapore. My work combines LBM-DEM, CFD-DEM, pore-network modelling, and machine learning to study fluid-particle systems, granular materials, and soft-clay stabilisation. I also develop Bayesian optimisation methods for the data-driven design of low-carbon binders.

I completed my PhD in Geotechnical Engineering at the University of Melbourne in 2025. I previously earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Civil Engineering at Beijing Jiaotong University.

Research Areas and Projects

  • Computational Modelling and Code Development – Irregular-particle DEM, LBM-DEM, CFD-DEM, pore-network modelling, CT image processing, and hydrodynamic manifold modelling.
  • AI and Machine Learning – Bayesian optimisation, graph-based simulation, transfer learning, Kolmogorov–Arnold networks, and convolutional autoencoders for engineering applications.
  • Particulate Materials and Geomaterials – Fluidised beds, internal erosion, coastal protection, tunnelling, and soft-clay stabilisation.
  • Low-Carbon Binder Design – Uncertainty-aware, closed-loop design of binders for clay stabilisation using Bayesian optimisation.
  • TiGeoNet – A Taichi-accelerated framework for pore-scale network extraction and complex network feature analysis.
  • Interactive-DEM – An interactive 2D discrete element method (DEM) web application for visualising particle interactions and mechanics. [Live Demo]
