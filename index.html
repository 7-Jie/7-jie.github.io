About Me

I completed my PhD in Geotechnical Engineering at the University of Melbourne in November 2025. My research focuses on fluid flow and transport behaviour in granular materials, combining particle-scale numerical simulations (CFD-DEM and LBM-DEM), pore-network modelling, and data-driven approaches including machine learning. I previously obtained my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in civil engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University, with a focus on tunnelling and underground engineering.

Research Works

  • Hydraulic Behaviour of Granular Assemblies – LBM-DEM modelling, 3D-printed experiments, CT scanning and image processing, and pore-network modelling for permeability and anisotropy analysis.
  • Internal Erosion in Gap-Graded Soils – Particle-scale CFD-DEM simulations combined with deep learning (3D convolutional autoencoders) for data-driven detection of erosion initiation.
  • Tunnelling Engineering – Tunnel face stability and seepage effects investigated using DEM simulations and numerical–theoretical analysis.
  • TiGeoNet – A Taichi-accelerated framework for pore-scale network extraction and complex network feature analysis.
  • Interactive-DEM – An interactive 2D discrete element method (DEM) web application for visualising particle interactions and mechanics. [Live Demo]
